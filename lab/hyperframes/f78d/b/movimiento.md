VIEJO

### cap 7 — igual

VIEJO

A slow, somber documentary shot on grainy 35mm film, muted colors and slight underexposure, seen from high on an upper balcony. The camera holds a locked-off wide shot with a subtle, slow downward drift, starting on the full street of ochre-brick blocks and ending slightly closer on the residents below. First the drying laundry on the metal balconies stirs gently in a light breeze, then the residents walk slowly along the pavement, one with an uneven, dragging step. Pale dawn light and long soft shadows stay steady over the quiet suburban street as the street lamps fade out. The residents keep to their paths, faces and bodies steady and natural.
