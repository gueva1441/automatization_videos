- **mining_workers** — who: Workers employed in the crocidolite asbestos mines and mill, including both local and migrant laborers. · where_when: Wittenoom mines and mill, predominantly 1937-1966, but especially in the 1950s-1960s.
- **european_migrant_families** — who: Families from European countries, particularly Spain, recruited to work and live in Wittenoom. · where_when: Arriving in Wittenoom, living in the town from the late 1940s through the mid-1960s.
- **wittenoom_children** — who: Children born or raised in Wittenoom, exposed to asbestos through play and daily life. · where_when: Playing in the town's patios, roads, and public spaces, 1950s-1960s.
- **officials_regulators** — who: Mine inspectors, state engineers, company representatives (e.g., CSR), and government officials. · where_when: Visiting the mine and town in various capacities from the 1940s to the 1960s.
- **asbestos_advocates_victims** — who: Former workers and residents suffering from asbestos-related diseases, and their legal/health advocates. · where_when: From the 1970s onwards, in Australia and elsewhere, involved in lawsuits and advocacy.
